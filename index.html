AXI4-Lite Peripheral on FPGA

RTL design and verification · April 2026

An AXI4-Lite peripheral subsystem on a Cmod A7-35T with MMCM clock generation, PWM, timer, and GPIO blocks, plus a UART to AXI4-Lite bridge using an async FIFO for clock domain crossing. The register model and the hardware in the loop pytest suite are generated from a single SystemRDL source, so RTL, firmware, and tests cannot drift apart. Root caused a UART bit period defect where a width truncation ahead of a subtraction corrupted the terminal count, and corrected it in both the receive and transmit paths.

SystemVerilog · Vivado · cocotb · Verilator · SystemRDL · Python · Artix-7

GoldBench

Quantitative forecasting benchmark · May 2026

A reproducible benchmark evaluating five model families on five years of daily gold futures across directional and volatility tracks, with a leakage safe feature pipeline and purged walk forward cross validation over 28 folds. Next day direction came out near chance at 0.515 AUC; volatility showed real signal at 0.276 ridge correlation. Exporting the LightGBM model to ONNX cut model size 34% and sped inference 2.8x with identical predictions.

Python · pandas · LightGBM · PyTorch · ONNX
