About me

Hi — I'm Nicholas, a senior at Rice. The question I keep coming back to is the one every hardware team has to answer: how do you know it works? I like building the machinery that answers it — register models generated from a single source of truth, characterization benches that sweep a part until it breaks, and CI that catches the regression before a human does.

Three summers at Rockwell Automation took me from drive firmware to production software to building hardware testbeds from a design doc. In between, I publish machine learning research with Baylor College of Medicine and lead hardware for Rice's OwlSat CubeSat. Off the clock I'm probably reworking a board I already called done.
