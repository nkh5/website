Hi! My name is Nicholas Hu, and I'm a senior at Rice University studying Electrical and Computer Engineering with a minor in Data Science.

I've spent three summers at Rockwell Automation, which took me from motor drive firmware to production Kotlin to building hardware testbeds from a design spec.

Alongside that I work on machine learning research with Rice ECE Department and Baylor College of Medicine, predicting depression severity from wearable and smartphone time series. We published in the Journal of Vascular Surgery in July 2026.

My side projects live where software meets silicon. I built an AXI4-Lite peripheral subsystem on an Artix-7 where the register model and the test suite are generated from the same source, so the spec and the tests cannot quietly disagree. I built a silicon characterization bench that sweeps voltage and frequency on three microcontroller families until they fail, then records exactly where. And I built GoldBench, a forecasting benchmark that I built and compared five different AI model families and optimized their size.

On campus I lead the hardware team for OwlSat, Rice's CubeSat club, and sit on the IEEE officer board. Off the clock I'll probably be skateboarding across campus.
